## Key Success Factors

1. **H1 includes city name** for local SEO
2. **First 100 words include keywords** (washer, dryer, repair, Fort Lauderdale)
3. **Multiple CTAs** above and below fold
4. **Trust signals** (licensed, insured, warranty)
5. **Internal links to all 8 city pages** for relevance
6. **FAQ with schema markup** for rich snippets
7. **Service areas listed** (location diversity)
8. **Events tracked in GA4** (clicks, forms, calls)
