# Google Business Profile Setup - Complete Guide

## Step 1: Access Google Business Profile

1. Go to: **google.com/business**
2. Sign in with Google account that owns Top Speed Appliance
3. Select your business location
4. Click **Edit Profile**

---

## Step 2: Set Primary Website Link

**Website Field:**
```
https://topspeedappliance.net
```

Or use main city page:
```

```

## Step 5: Enable Google Posts

**Frequency:** Post 1–2 times per week

**Post Types:**
- Service spotlight (Washer, Dryer, Refrigerator, Oven)
- Before/after photos
- Special offers
- Seasonal maintenance tips
- Emergency service highlights

**Example Post Format:**
```
Title: Same-Day Washer Repair in Fort Lauderdale
Text: Washer not spinning? Water leaking? Call Top Speed Appliance for fast, same-day repair. Licensed & insured. Call (954) 931-7997
CTA Button: Call Now
Link: /washer-repair?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp_washer
```

## Step 9: Track Performance in GA4

**After 1 Week:**
1. Check GA4 Reports → Acquisition → Traffic Acquisition
2. Look for Source = "google", Medium = "organic"
3. Filter by Campaign = "gbp_washer" | "gbp_dryer" | etc.
4. Track clicks and conversions by service

**Expected Results:**
- Week 1–2: Traffic starts showing as "organic" instead of "direct"
- Week 3–4: Click patterns emerge (which services get most clicks)
- Month 2: SEO rankings improve for local searches
